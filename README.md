## About Oncology Treatment

Cancer treatment is planned according to the type and stage of cancer, medical history, diagnostic findings, and the patient's overall treatment requirements. Depending on the condition, treatment may involve surgery, chemotherapy, radiation therapy, targeted treatment, immunotherapy, or a combination of different approaches.

## Oncology Treatment for International Patients

International patients planning cancer treatment in India can share their medical reports, scans, biopsy results, and previous treatment records with an oncology specialist before travelling. This can help the medical team review the available information and determine the next steps for evaluation and treatment.

Patients should also consider consultations, treatment duration, accommodation, travel arrangements, and follow-up care when planning their medical journey.
